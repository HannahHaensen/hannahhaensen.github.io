CoRe-GS: Coarse-to-Refined Gaussian Splatting with Semantic Object Focus
H. Schieber, D. Frischmann, S. Boche, V. Schaack, A. Schoellig, S. Leutenegger, D. Roth

Preprint · 2026
Abstract
Project Page
ArXiv

Mobile reconstruction for autonomous aerial robotics holds strong potential for critical applications such as tele-guidance and disaster response. These tasks demand accurate 3D reconstruction and fast scene processing. We propose CoRe-GS, a coarse-to-refined semantic Gaussian Splatting approach that focuses reconstruction on semantically relevant points of interest, reducing training time.


Locality-Sensitive Hashing for Efficient Hard Negative Contrastive Learning
F. Deuser, P. Hausenblas, H. Schieber, D. Roth, M. Werner, N. Oswald

Preprint · 2025
Abstract
ArXiv

We propose a GPU-friendly Locality-Sensitive Hashing (LSH) scheme for efficient hard negative mining in contrastive learning. Our method quantizes real-valued feature vectors into binary representations enabling approximate nearest neighbor search. It achieves comparable or better performance with significantly reduced computational cost across textual and visual datasets.
